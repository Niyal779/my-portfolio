02 — About Me

Who I Am

Niyal Rahaman

Software Engineer

My name is Niyal Rahaman, and I am a passionate and driven Computer Science & Engineering student at American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). From a young age, I have been fascinated by how technology shapes the world — and that curiosity led me to pursue software engineering as my calling.

I specialize in full-stack web development, working with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL to craft responsive, user-friendly digital experiences. Beyond the web, I have explored desktop application development in Java (GUI/Swing) and C# (.NET), as well as system-level programming in C and C++.

" I believe great software is not just about writing code — it's about solving real problems with clarity, creativity, and craftsmanship. "

My academic journey has given me a strong foundation in Data Structures, Algorithms, OOP, and Database Management. I have also explored the engineering side of technology through AutoCAD, MATLAB, Simulink, and Arduino-based embedded systems — which gives me a unique cross-disciplinary perspective.

I have built real-world projects ranging from a Product Management System in C#, an Online Jersey Shop in Java, to browser-based games in JavaScript. Each project has sharpened my problem-solving skills and deepened my understanding of software architecture.
